⭐ Week 1 · Self-Paced Learning
Welcome to Five Star Bath Solutions Sales Training
  • What kind of company did you just agree to work for?
  • What is our role when we enter a home?
  • How are we different from a typical one-call-close company?
  • Who are our customers, and why did they call us?
  • What exactly is it that we sell?
  • How do you price it out and create a vision for the customer?

Week 1 is going to answer all of those questions.

Before we get into the strategy and process of Week 2, we want you brought fully up to speed on the basics — who we are, what we sell, how we operate, and what it means to represent Five Star Bath Solutions in the home.

This is self-paced learning. Work through each page at your own speed, but stay with it. Each page builds on the last. You'll also be joining a daily one-hour check-in with your training coaches via Google Meet — that's where you bring your questions and your coaches walk you through anything you need.

As you work through this content, you'll see two types of action items. Exercises are reflection questions built into each page — answer them as you go. At the end of Day 1 you'll submit everything together.
