🦸 Our Philosophy
The Customer Hero Movement
This is the philosophy that separates Five Star Bath Solutions from every other home improvement company. Learn it. Own it. Let it change how you show up in every home.
The Core Idea
"In every home you enter, the customer is the hero. You are the guide."
Most sales training teaches you to be the hero — the expert who swoops in with the solution. We teach something different. The customer arrived at this moment with a problem, a dream, and a story. Your job is to understand that story and help them write the next chapter. When you do that well, the sale takes care of itself.
