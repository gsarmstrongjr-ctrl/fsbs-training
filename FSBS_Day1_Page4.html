What made you decide to join Five Star?
I was self-employed for many years and getting burnt out with work/life balance. FSB has been a joy — helping customers update their bathrooms or make them safer so they can stay in their home longer is rewarding in many different ways.
How has the sales system changed how you connect?
Putting customers at the center of the story instead of talking all about the company was a change. I have noticed that customers feel more comfortable, and less resistant to the sales process.
One moment that really stuck with you?
A nice lady who was getting taken advantage of by one of our competitors. We were able to take care of her with a much better solution. She was so happy and grateful that she became emotional — and it felt very rewarding.
What would you say to someone thinking about joining?
Five Star Bath has been a wonderful experience. Our products and warranties are exactly what customers actually need. Our selling process feels very natural.
