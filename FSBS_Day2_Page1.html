Field Notes — Backer Board
Key concepts from the Backer Board Mini Manual
Our Standard: ShowerShield Foam Core
  • Closed-cell EPS foam — lightweight, strong, and fully waterproof
  • Installed with high-adhesion wall foam and mechanical fasteners
  • Proprietary and exclusive to our system — competitors can't match it
  • Adds insulation, sound dampening, and dimensional stability
How It Compares
Cement Board (Hardie, Durock)
Heavy, absorbs moisture, can crumble. Requires a separate vapor barrier — not waterproof on its own.
Greenboard / Drywall
Still a drywall product. What low-cost companies still use. Absorbs moisture over time and fails in real wet-area conditions.
Purple Board
Moisture-resistant, but not waterproof. Risk of failure in sustained wet exposure.
ShowerShield (Ours)
Fully waterproof. No vapor barrier needed. Warmer, quieter shower. The gold standard — and exclusive to us.
Why Customers Should Care
  • Better mold and mildew protection — nothing gets behind the wall
  • Quieter, warmer shower experience from day one
  • No soggy walls hiding behind beautiful panels
  • Premium construction equals lasting value
"What's behind the wall is just as important as what you see. Our closed-cell foam board gives you insulation, sound control, and total waterproofing — so this shower will feel solid for decades."
