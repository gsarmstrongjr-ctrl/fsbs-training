Field Notes — Accessories
Glass, Faucets, Grab Bars, Seats & Storage
Glass Doors
  • Sliding glass (standard or barn-style) — non-custom, available from stock
  • Swing doors and neo-angle doors — measured and ordered post-sale (custom)
  • Curtain rods included when no door is chosen
  • Always verify orientation and swing direction during the sit
Faucets — Delta 1700 Series
  • Our standard: Delta 1700 series — durable, proven, and stylish
  • In2ition style (handheld nested in showerhead) is our most popular
  • Chrome is standard; other finishes available for an added charge
Grab Bars, Seats & Shelving
Grab Bars
Multiple sizes and finishes. Installed into studs when possible. Ideal at entry points, near seats, or where customer demonstrates need.
Seats
Fold-down seats and built-in benches. Size must match shower layout — confirm during design.
Shave Steps
Small corner-mounted ledges for leg support. Low-profile and popular in smaller showers.
Shelves & Niches
Corner shelves or recessed niches. Niches must be scoped properly — avoid exterior walls.
Accessories must be scoped during the sit. Confirm layout, orientation, and finish details. Every detail should be part of the design — no surprises post-sale.

What the Warranty Covers
And how to use it in the conversation
What It Covers
  • Full lifetime guarantee on all parts and materials included on Smart Choice and Premium packages
  • If any defect in material or installation is found, Five Star Bath replaces it at no charge
  • Transferable to the new owner of the property — adds resale value
What It Doesn't Cover
  • Customer-provided materials are not covered under our warranty
  • Damage caused by misuse or modification after installation
Don't hide the warranty — lead with it. Most customers have been burned by companies that don't stand behind their work. Our guarantee is proof we do.
