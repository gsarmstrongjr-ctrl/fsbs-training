Field Notes — The 7-Step Post-Appointment System
Your appointment isn't over until you drive away
1
Professional Exit — Regardless of Outcome
Thank the customer sincerely. Maintain positive demeanor. Leave gracefully with a smile. Your exit is their final impression of Five Star.
2
Organize Your Mobile Office
Sample kit returned to proper place. Documents filed. Business cards restocked. Organized car = organized mind.
3
Complete Documentation BEFORE Driving
Send result notifications. Update appointment status. Record outcome and notes while fresh. Golden Rule: Put nothing in drive until documentation is complete.
4
Stay Available — The Magic Happens Here
Customers sometimes have second thoughts after you leave. Stay 5–10 minutes. Some of our best sales happen when the rep is still in the driveway.
5
Think Like a Business Owner
Track daily: appointments, sales, time, miles. Track weekly: closing %, average sale, commission. You are the CEO of your territory.
6
Emotional Detachment Through Data
Write it down, let it go. Recording results removes emotional attachment. Each appointment becomes one data point — not a referendum on your worth.
7
Mental Reset for Next Appointment
Previous appointment fully documented. Mind clear and focused forward. Review next customer info. Set positive intention. Complete closure creates a fresh start.
"Finish strong. Document smart. Move forward clear."
