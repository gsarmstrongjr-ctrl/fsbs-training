Field Notes — Professionalism Standards
What it looks like to represent the brand with excellence
The Five Star Standard
✓ Five Star Reps Do
  • Arrive fully prepared and early
  • Wear clean, pressed Five Star logo shirt
  • iPad charged to 100% before every sit
  • Organized, clutter-free vehicle
  • Focus solely on customer needs
  • Use reliable, professional tools
✗ Trust Killers
  • Dead or uncharged devices
  • Wrinkled or stained clothing
  • Disorganized or missing materials
  • Bringing personal problems to appointments
  • Messy or poorly maintained vehicle
  • Rushing or appearing scattered
"If they can't maintain their own vehicle, can they handle my bathroom project?" — How customers think. Your car is a rolling billboard.

Field Notes — The Four Pillars of Five Star Confidence
Internal confidence vs. performed confidence
The Four Pillars
  • Trust the Product — Smart Choice is genuinely superior. Engineered for performance. Backed by a comprehensive warranty. Proven across thousands of installations.
  • Trust the Process — The S.T.O.R.Y. system works when followed. Customer-centered approach builds trust. Education creates confident decisions. No pressure needed.
  • Trust the Company — Industry leader. Standing behind work and warranties. Continuous improvement. Values-driven.
  • Trust Yourself — You've been trained in proven systems. You represent something excellent. Your preparation shows professionalism. You're helping customers make good decisions.
Internal vs. Performed Confidence
✓ Internal (What We Want)
  • Calm assurance
  • Genuine belief in product and process
  • Natural trust-building
  • Authentic communication
  • Comfortable with silence
✗ Performed (What to Avoid)
  • Fake energy or forced enthusiasm
  • Aggressive closing tactics
  • Manufactured urgency
  • Defensive about pricing
  • Avoiding difficult questions
"Con + Fidere = with trust. Real confidence is integrity that shines through when you know what you offer is genuinely superior."
